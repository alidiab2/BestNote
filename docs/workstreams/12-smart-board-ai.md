# WS-12 Smart Board AI (`AI`)

**Goal:** photograph a whiteboard and get the notes the best student in the room would have written. That means logical order, ruler-straight diagrams, meaningful colors and clean LaTeX, not an OCR dump.
**Owns:** `:ai:vision-api`, `:ai:math`, `:engine:layout`, future provider modules (`:ai:vision-gemini`, …)
**Decision:** [ADR-0007 Optional cloud AI](../architecture/adr/0007-optional-cloud-ai.md)

## The mental model: "the expert student note-taker"
The model is told it is an elite, meticulous student copying the board into a notebook (`Prompts.EXPERT_STUDENT`). Each of the four behaviors is also enforced **deterministically** in `:engine:layout`, so notes stay clean even when the model is only partly right:

| Behavior | In the prompt | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| **Lecture flow over board layout:** copy in teaching order, with a hierarchy of title → definitions → proofs → key formulas → margin notes | `order`, block types `definition`, `proof`, `margin_note`, `boxed` | `LectureFlow` (explicit order, else panel-by-panel reading, RTL aware; margin notes follow their anchor), `BlockFlow` (headings/definitions keep-with-next, margin notes aligned beside their block) |
| **Ruler and compass geometry** | "lines straight, angles exact, arrows anchored" | `RulerGeometry`: weld endpoints, square H/V, snap 45°/15°, close polygons, round circles, anchor arrows on outlines, half-point grid |
| **Color-coded semantics** | `ink_color` + `semantic` (regular/essential/annotation/emphasis) | `ColorSemantics` (names + hue), `NotebookPalette` (one coherent palette; essential formulas boxed) |
| **Self-correction and polish** | fix slips, bullets, LaTeX, keep the lecture's language | `LatexSanitizer` (delimiters, Unicode → LaTeX, safety, balance), `DocumentMapper` (drops invalid blocks, never fails the whole page) |

## Status (AI-04..07 first cut)
- `:engine:handwriting` writes blocks as timed ink: Hershey print/script hands, Aref Ruqaa forms for Arabic, LaTeX subset (fractions, scripts, roots, Greek, operators), pen-drawn boxes, ruler diagrams, green margin notes with an arrow. `PageComposer.measurer` is the AI-06 measurer.
- `RuledFlow` + `RuledPaper.of(template, size)` put every line of writing on the editor's own ruled lines, inside the margin.
- `:feature:smartboard`: photo (camera or picker) → crop → provider (Gemini with the user's key, else the offline `DemoVisionProvider`) → review page that writes itself → inserted as new pages, one undo step.
- AI-11: any provider answer is parsed by `AiDocumentParser` (fences, prose, truncation, bad escapes, aliases) and mapped by `DocumentMapper`, which salvages text from alternate fields and records one reason per dropped block (`VisionDiagnostics`, Settings → Debug). Models are never hard-wired: `ModelCatalog` picks the newest pro-class (Best) or fast model from each provider's live list.
- AI-04 (`:ai:board-scan`, pure JVM, no OpenCV/Play Services): `BoardDetector` (downscaled Sobel edge map + Otsu luma split → regions grown from the centre → convex hull → largest inscribed quad), `Perspective` (homography warp), `Enhance` (per-channel background envelope divides out shadows, glare and colour casts; blackboards keep a dark ground). Capture: camera, gallery (several photos), one draggable handle per corner, "Preview" shows exactly what the AI reads. Multi-photo boards are read photo by photo and merged by `BoardMerge`.
- Review: paper picker (ruled, narrow, wide, grid, dotted, blank, Cornell; `RuledPaper.of` for every writable template), preview drawn with the editor's own template geometry, insert after the page Smart Board was opened from (pages + ink one undo step, `PagedDocument.insertPages`) or into a new notebook.
- Writing: word-level bidi runs (English and numbers stay readable inside Arabic lines, digits keep their order inside Arabic words), inline `\( … \)` maths written with the maths layout instead of flattened text, proof reasons beside their step, a blank line under hanging fractions, one pen per role (headings emphasis, boxed formulas red, margin notes green).
- AI-08 Tidy: lasso → Tidy sends the selection image to the chosen provider (`beautify`), or uses the on-device recogniser without a key, and writes the result neatly on the page's ruled lines in the selection's colour; one undo step.
- Font output (default, owner's direction): `FontComposer` writes the notes as `TextElement`s in bundled OFL handwriting fonts (Caveat for Latin/Cyrillic, Aref Ruqaa for Arabic; Hebrew and other scripts use the device font until an OFL Hebrew hand is bundled) on the same ruled-line layout, with maths laid out as real maths (fractions, scripts, roots) from small text runs plus pen strokes, and pen strokes for underlines, boxes, highlights, arrows and drawings. "Pen strokes" (the synthetic hand) stays as an option.
- Design styles (`StyleTemplate` + `StyleTemplates`: Classic, Minimal, Colorful, Cornell, Cheat sheet): one fixed colour, size and decoration per `NoteRole` (title, heading, definition, theorem, formula, result, example, step, note, warning, margin), paper and spacing. Picked in review with sketches and a colour legend; saved per notebook (`SmartBoardStyles`) with an app-wide default in Settings.
- Languages: per-block direction from the block's own letters (Hebrew and Arabic right to left, English and maths left to right inside them), Hebrew/Arabic/English labels, `lang` in the schema, and a notes language ("same as the board" or translate into English, Hebrew, Arabic, French, Spanish, German, Russian, Turkish) saved per notebook, also used by Tidy. Convert with AI reads Hebrew.
- Parser: single-backslash LaTeX (`\frac` → form feed) is protected before JSON parsing; boolean flags never become text.
- Left for later: an OFL Hebrew handwriting font (needs a download the owner approves), cheat-sheet/minimal AI layouts, the user's own hand ("Mine"), live camera edge preview (capture uses the system camera app), AI-09 scoring.

## Templates first (TPL-01)
- The template comes FIRST (`NoteTemplate`: look + `AiInstructions` + allowed `NoteElement`s), chosen before capture with compact chips and saved per notebook. The AI only picks elements and fills them; the app guarantees layout quality.
- Wire format: `{"elements":[{"e":…,"t","h","m","items","steps":[{"s","m","why"}],"rows","terms","target","n","cols","parts","fbd"/"flow"/"axes"}],"terms":[[…]]}` — no coordinates, colours, sizes, source boxes or ids. The old block format still parses (`LegacyNotes`). Measured on the demo lecture: prompt + schema ≈2,080 → ≈890 tokens (−58%), answer ≈490 → ≈280 tokens (−43%) (`NoteWireTest`).
- `NoteNormalizer` (enforced, whatever the model wrote): only the template's elements; in Hebrew/Arabic sentences only short atoms stay inline (`x`, `e^x`, `30!`), fractions/limits/sums/roots/multi-term maths move to their own centred Formula line; numbered formulas ①②…; the page's new glossary terms at its end. Inline atoms that are tall get extra lines (`FontComposer.inlineExtent`).
- Study elements (reference: a friend's Hebrew physics notes): `Columns` (dashed divider, first column on the right in RTL), `NumberedFormula`, `VariableLegend`, `Derivation` (aligned at "="), `WorkedExample` (given values with units, sub-questions א ב ג / a b c / أ ب ج, boxed results), `StarNote`; diagrams by data (`DiagramKinds`: free-body, flowchart, axes). Built-in template "Study notes (columns)".
- Storage: `NotebookPackage.extras` (format 5) — `smartboard.json` holds the notebook glossary and, per insert, template id, page ids, the notes and fingerprints of Smart Board's own ink. A follow-up ("Continue / add photo" on a Smart Board page) sends the page's elements with the new photo, the model returns one merged list (`NoteMerge.dedupe` as a safety net), and the pages are re-laid out around the user's own ink (`ReservedLines`) in one undo step (`PagedDocument.asOneStep`).
- For the template editor: `NoteLayout.previewPage(template, measure, sampleDoc)` and `NoteSamples.forTemplate(template)`.

## Output style (agreed with the maintainer)
The result must look like the user wrote it in their own notebook, only very neat and very fast. It must never look like rendered Markdown or typeset documents. See `design/` → Smart Board.

## Rules
- Any prompt change must keep `PromptsTest` green and be scored on the AI-09 evaluation set.
- Board colors never reach the page directly. They always go through `InkSemantic` → `NotebookPalette`.
- Never fail a whole reconstruction because of one bad block. Drop it and count it.

## Dense notebook layout (TPL-03)
- Owner feedback (DeepSeek, Arabic notes, grid paper): one sparse column, density depended on the AI choosing Columns (it never did). Target: a friend's Hebrew physics notebook — many things on one page, dashed lines between topics, organised.
- `StyleTemplate.layout` (`Flow` | `Packed`) and `density` (`Airy` | `Normal` | `Dense`); Classic, Minimal, Colorful, Cheat sheet and Study notes are Packed (Cheat sheet and Study notes Dense), Cornell stays Flow. Saved in template JSON; chips in the template editor.
- `PackedFlow` (`:engine:layout`, pure): a Heading starts a topic; column blocks flow newspaper-style through 2 columns (3 for Dense on landscape pages), the first column on the start side (right in RTL); the title and 3+-column tables span the page and close a balanced section; rows split between columns only at whole rows with keep rules (headings keep the next block's start, short/boxed blocks stay whole, no lone first/last line, pen boxes glued to their rows); dashed vertical rule between columns, dashed horizontal rule between topics and sections; the user's own lines are skipped.
- `NoteLayout` packed path: grid/dot paper writes on every grid line (Normal/Dense), smaller display maths and steps, short equations share a line (`PackedNotes`: "a = 1,  b = −1"), a Columns element's contents join the flow, margin remarks become ★ notes.
- Rendering: `\begin{cases|aligned|array|pmatrix…}`, `\left…\right` and `|x|` bars as pen strokes, limits under lim/Σ, `\overset`; bare LaTeX in table cells is maths; lines are ordered with `java.text.Bidi` and split per script (Arabic and Hebrew in one title each get their own hand); glossary pairs that repeat the notes' word are dropped (no glossary when none differs), a term's bracketed translation is not repeated.
- Visual check without AI calls: `NotebookRenderTest` (engine fixtures, incl. the owner's partial-fractions board) and `LiveNotesRenderTest` (three recorded DeepSeek answers in `feature/smartboard/src/test/resources/notes/`) write before/after PNGs; samples in `docs/qa/smartboard/tpl03-*`.
