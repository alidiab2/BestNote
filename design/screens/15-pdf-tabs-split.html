Torque & the lever armPHYS 201 · Lecture 7 · Slide 12
Torque measures how strongly a force rotates an object. It depends on where and in which direction you push.
τ = r × F, |τ| = r F sin φ
r: from the pivot to where the force acts φ: angle between r and F Lever arm r⊥ = r sin φ, so τ = r⊥ F. Counter-clockwise torque is positive by convention.
