We made you a first notebook. Three moves worth knowing:

Hold the S Pen buttonTemporary eraser. Let go to keep writing.
Two-finger tap to undoThree fingers to redo.

Motion: steps advance with Spring.Gentle (content slides, the visual cross-fades). The welcome handwriting is an illustration, so it may animate. Real ink never does. Every step can be skipped; all choices live in Settings later.
